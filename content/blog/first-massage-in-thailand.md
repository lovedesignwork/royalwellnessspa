## You do not need to know the routine in advance

For your first massage in Thailand, choose a clearly described treatment, tell the spa that it is your first visit and explain your preferred pressure. Ask about clothing and draping before the session begins. You can request a change or stop at any point. A comfortable first appointment starts with understanding what will happen, rather than trying to behave like an experienced spa guest.

Phuket offers many different massage experiences, and the word “massage” alone does not tell you which one you are booking. Traditional Thai massage often involves clothed pressure and assisted stretching; oil massage uses gliding contact on the skin. A combined ritual may include a scrub, facial or other stages. Begin with the format that sounds most comfortable to you.

## Choose one clear starting point

If you enjoy the idea of assisted movement, ask about Royal Thai Balance. If you prefer flowing strokes and an oil-based session, explore Aroma Relaxation Massage. If you are drawn to warmth, ask about Thai Herbal Compress Therapy. You do not need to try several unfamiliar techniques in the same visit simply to make the most of being in Thailand.

Read the treatment inclusions and duration, then ask one or two practical questions. “Does this involve strong stretching?” or “Can we begin with light pressure?” will help you choose. Our [Thai and oil massage comparison](/blog/thai-massage-vs-oil-massage) explains the differences in more detail.

For a first visit, leave enough time around the appointment to feel settled. A shorter session that fits your day comfortably can be a better personal choice than a long package squeezed between transport and dinner. Confirm the overall time to allow, including consultation and getting ready.

## Before you arrive

Check the address, appointment time and treatment name. Royal Wellness Spa is on the third floor of Royal Phuket City Hotel in Phuket Town. The [contact page](/contact) has location information, and the team can help clarify arrival details if you are unfamiliar with the area.

Wear comfortable clothing that is easy to change out of if necessary. Ask whether your selected treatment uses provided clothing or your own, rather than making assumptions from the word “Thai.” Keep jewelry and valuables manageable so you do not have to sort through many small items just before relaxing.

Tell the team in advance about injuries, pregnancy, health conditions, relevant medication and allergies or sensitivities. If a medical issue makes you unsure about massage, ask your healthcare professional about suitability first. A spa can explain what it offers, but it should not be asked to diagnose the cause of a symptom.

## Use the consultation to set expectations

The most useful starting information is simple: this is your first massage, this is the pressure you think you would enjoy, and these are the areas you want treated gently or avoided. It is fine if you do not know your exact preference yet. Ask to start lightly and adjust as you discover what feels comfortable.

You can use phrases such as:

- “This is my first massage. Could you explain the main steps?”
- “Please start gently and check with me before increasing pressure.”
- “I would prefer no stretching of this area.”
- “I am sensitive to fragrance. Which product will you use?”
- “Could you explain the changing and draping arrangements?”

Ask before the treatment begins if you are unsure about any part of the process. Clarifying a practical detail is not rude or disruptive. It makes it easier to settle once the session starts.

## Clothing, privacy and personal boundaries

Different treatments need different arrangements. A clothed massage needs clothing that allows comfortable movement. An oil treatment involves discussion of changing and draping. Rather than following a rule remembered from another spa, ask what is appropriate for the session you have selected.

Only proceed with arrangements you understand and are comfortable with. If you prefer to keep particular clothing on or avoid an area, explain this before beginning and ask whether the treatment can be adapted. The answer may affect which service is suitable, and it is better to know that early.

The same principle applies during the massage. You can ask for a towel to be adjusted, a position to change or a technique to stop. Your preferences do not become fixed simply because you agreed to the appointment at reception.

## Speak up while the session is happening

A therapist cannot reliably infer your comfort from silence. Say “less pressure, please” when you need less, rather than waiting for a difficult movement to finish. If you want to understand what comes next, ask. You can also say that you would prefer a quiet session once the practical questions are settled.

You do not need to hold your breath, tense against a movement or endure pain to be a good guest. Communicate clearly and let the therapist respond. If you remain uncomfortable, ask to stop the technique or the session. Comfort is more important than completing every listed step.

Afterward, take a moment before rushing into your next activity. Ask any remaining questions about the products used or the treatment you received. Notice what you liked: the pressure, the style, the pace or the focus area. Those observations will make a future booking much easier to describe.

## Common first-visit questions

### Should I know the name of every technique?

No. Ordinary language is useful. Explain whether you prefer oil, stretching, warmth, gentle pressure or a particular focus area, and ask the team to describe unfamiliar terms.

### What if my partner likes much stronger massage?

Your preferences can differ. State them separately, even when you have booked a shared experience. Read our [couples’ massage guide](/blog/couples-massage-phuket-town) for questions to ask before booking together.

### Is it acceptable to change my mind about pressure?

Yes. Your initial request is a starting point, and different areas can feel different. Ask for an adjustment when you need one.

### How do I reserve a first visit?

Browse the [treatment menu](/treatments), choose an experience that appeals to you and [book your appointment](/book). Mention that it is your first massage and include any preferences the team should know beforehand.
